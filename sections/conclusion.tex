\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

In this pipeline, how well a graph answered held-out questions followed what reached it. A relation list induced from the corpus, without gold relations, raised answer correctness by 16.3 points on 2WikiMultihopQA and 12.5 on T-REx, and lists induced only outside the evaluation documents also raised grounded QA on both corpora. Once the extractor had a relation list, extraction from every chunk without CQs matched the combined pipeline in grounded QA on the multi-hop corpus, with fewer tokens than the combined pipeline's first pass alone. Without any list it scored above iText2KG, EDC and AutoSchemaKG, and with the list it scored 15.8 to 39.2 points above them. At equal fact count, the CQ-guided single pass scored above a random subset of the listed extraction and above heuristic, chain-preserving, model-scored, CQ-based and chunk-balanced selections. A corpus-induced relation vocabulary with extraction from every chunk is a simple, strong baseline for CQ-driven graph construction, and with a fixed fact count CQ guidance kept more answerable facts than random and question-agnostic subsets.
